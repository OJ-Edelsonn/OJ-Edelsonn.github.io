## The challenge

Build a consulting case that connects commercial and inventory signals with prioritized actions, without presenting analytical relationships as real-world outcomes.

## Approach

The work uses simulated data with a realistic business structure and public industry context. The diagnosis examines sales, margin, rotation, stock, and overstock before creating scenarios.

## Solution

The proposal organizes opportunities by product and recommended action. It then calculates conservative, moderate, and aggressive scenarios to estimate potentially released capital and annual operating savings.

## Findings within the simulated case

The simulation records sales growth from S/ 273.81 million in 2023 to S/ 339.83 million in 2025 and S/ 34.38 million in overstock value. In the moderate scenario, a 40% overstock reduction is associated with S/ 13.75 million in potentially released capital and S/ 1.65 million in reference annual operating savings.

## Technical decisions

- Separate diagnosis, prioritization, and impact simulation.
- Keep assumptions and outputs in auditable tabular files.
- Label every visual and figure as simulated.
