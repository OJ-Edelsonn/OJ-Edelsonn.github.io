## The challenge

Turn a premium pet-food idea into a coherent business proposition with clear differentiation and a responsible view of the Peruvian market.

## Approach

The team defined the business concept, segments, value proposition, and functional product lines. It then contrasted the idea against competitors, industry forces, external factors, and economic, political, social, and technology scenarios.

## Academic result

The solution combines a Business Model Canvas, Competitive Profile Matrix, EFE, and POAM. Recommendations prioritize veterinary validation, traceability, partnerships with specialist channels, trial packs, and communication without medical claims.

## Learning outcomes

- Connecting a value proposition with environmental evidence and entry decisions.
- Separating commercial opportunity from technical and regulatory feasibility.
- Presenting quantitative matrices as support rather than certainty.

## Next steps

Validate the problem with customers, consult specialists, estimate actual costs, and review health requirements before proposing a pilot.
