## The challenge

Visually represent how company roles relate to processes and operational outcomes within a business architecture.

## Approach

The reviewed model connects a logistics supervisor with national distribution and a production manager with native-potato chip production. Each process generates or provides a business outcome.

## Academic value

The exercise demonstrates the core ArchiMate logic: separating actors, behavior, and objects while expressing their relationships through a common notation.

## Scope and correction

Evidence corresponds to Inka Chips. An Inkafarma BPMN diagram found in a different course is a separate assignment and is not used as a substitute for this case.

## Next steps
