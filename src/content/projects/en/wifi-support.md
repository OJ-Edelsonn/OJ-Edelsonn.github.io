## The challenge

Clearly present a wireless internet service and its main attributes through simple web navigation.

## Approach

The prototype organizes institutional information, services, coverage, features, and contact. Reviewed material includes an HTML page, image assets, and a screenshot of the visual model.

## Verifiable scope

Evidence confirms a static front-end exercise. It does not support claims that the service, forms, or commercial channels operated in production.

## Learning outcomes

- Defining a basic content hierarchy for a service.
- Building navigation and informational sections with HTML and CSS.
- Distinguishing an academic mock-up from a transactional solution.

## Next steps

Update the design, review accessibility, normalize file chronology, and document authorship.
