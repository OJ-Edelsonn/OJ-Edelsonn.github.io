## The challenge

Understand how a large-scale logistics operation connects suppliers, materials, distribution centers, routes, and technology to maintain service levels across sectors.

## Approach

The case organizes DHL Peru’s supply chain into five fronts: sourcing, inventory, warehousing, distribution, and transportation. It adds Lean principles, traceability technologies, and external-environment matrices.

## Value of the analysis

The documentation supports an executive reading of logistics as a system rather than a collection of isolated activities. It highlights the relationship among layout, standardization, operational visibility, and transportation decisions.

## Learning outcomes

- Connecting end-to-end processes within a supply chain.
- Distinguishing secondary-source evidence from original results.
- Applying business-analysis tools to an operational context.
