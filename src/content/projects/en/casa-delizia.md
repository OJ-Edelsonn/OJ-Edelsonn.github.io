## The challenge

Design a digital presence for an academic bakery case that presents the brand, supports product-category browsing, and facilitates contact across devices.

## Approach

The team structured the experience around a home page and four catalog paths: products, traditional bakery, gourmet bakery, and desserts. Navigation and styling remain consistent across pages.

## Solution

The site combines HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Bootstrap. Reviewed files include source code, images, menus, scope documentation, and a content structure for a small-business context.

## Learning outcomes

- Organizing commercial information in a simple navigation architecture.
- Reusing visual patterns across static pages.
- Separating a front-end prototype from capabilities that require a backend.

## Next steps

Improve accessibility, optimize assets, replace fictional data, and connect forms only after defining a secure backend.
